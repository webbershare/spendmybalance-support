Spend My Balance support

Compare optional developer-tip purchases using current App Store prices in mainland China (CNY) or the US (USD), or use the free Apple Support guide. No currency conversion is performed. Prices are estimates: taxes, payment eligibility and the final charge are determined by Apple. We cannot read your balance or guarantee it reaches zero.

Purchase stuck or result unclear?

Keep the app installed. Use “Sync Apple transactions”, then check Apple purchase history and your actual balance. Do not repeat a payment whose outcome is unknown. Ask to Buy requests need approval. If the parent or Apple confirms a request was declined or cancelled, use “Was the request declined or cancelled?” and review the warning before resetting your plan. This does not cancel an Apple request; an outstanding request may still be approved and charged later.

Refund or account issue?

Apple handles refund requests and Apple Account issues. Visit Apple Support. Refund eligibility is determined by Apple. Purchases in this app are voluntary consumable tips, not withdrawals or transfers.

Changing regions

A zero balance is only one condition. Review subscriptions, pending orders, refunds and Family Sharing in Apple's official guide.

Contact the developer

gdutwebber@gmail.com. Include the app version, iOS version, your language and a description of the issue. Never send your Apple password, verification codes or card details.

余额清零支持

你可以按中国大陆（人民币）或美国（美元）商店当前价格比较自愿支持开发者的购买方案，也可以使用免费的 Apple 支持指引。不进行汇率换算。税费、可用付款方式及最终扣款由 Apple 决定。我们不能读取余额，也不能保证清零。

购买卡住或结果不确定

请保留 App，先使用「同步 Apple 交易」，再核对 Apple 购买记录与实际余额。结果不明时请勿重复付款。「购买前询问」需要批准。只有家长或 Apple 已确认请求被拒绝或取消时，才可选择「请求已被拒绝或取消？」并阅读提示后重置方案。这不能取消 Apple 请求；未取消的请求以后仍可能获批扣款。

退款或账户问题

请通过上方 Apple 支持链接处理。退款资格由 Apple 决定。App 内购是自愿支持开发者的消耗型商品，不是提现或转账服务。

准备换区

清零只是换区条件之一，还需要核对订阅、待处理订单、退款和家庭共享，具体以上方 Apple 官方说明为准。

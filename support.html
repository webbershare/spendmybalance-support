Spend My Balance support

A free manual credit journal and region-preparation checklist. No login or new in-app purchases are required. It cannot read your Apple Account balance or change your region.

Record and check your balance

In Balance, choose Start a journal, select USD or CNY and enter your current balance. Currency is fixed once saved. Add top-ups, spending and refunds; record only changes to Apple Account credit, not amounts paid by card. Check actual balance compares your manual input with the estimate and saves the difference. Undo latest record corrects the last local entry without reversing any Apple transaction. Export CSV saves a copy of the journal; preparation tasks are not included.

Prepare and set reminders

In Prepare, set a destination, edit task notes and due dates, or add custom tasks. Completing or deleting a task cancels its pending reminder. If notification access is denied, turn off the reminder to save the task without it, or enable notifications in system Settings. Due tasks remain visible in the app. Checklist completion does not confirm eligibility; consult Apple's current requirements.

Earlier purchases or unresolved requests

This version does not start new payments. Earlier receipts remain separate from your journal. Keep the app installed, open Settings and use Sync earlier transactions, then check Apple purchase history. An earlier pending request may still be approved and charged. Archive a pending plan only after the parent or Apple confirms rejection or cancellation; archiving does not cancel or refund a transaction.

Data or account problems

If records cannot be read, keep the app installed and contact the developer; do not delete it to repair the data. If a save fails, check device storage and retry. Local data is not synced or included in iCloud backup. Apple handles account, balance and refund questions: Apple Support.

Contact the developer

gdutwebber@gmail.com. Include app version, iOS version, language and a description. Never send passwords, verification codes or card details.

余额清零支持

免费本机余额账本和换区准备清单，无需登录，本版不提供新的内购。不读取 Apple 账户余额，也不能替你换区。

记录与核对

在「余额记录」点击「开始记录」，选择人民币或美元并填写初始余额。保存后币种固定。添加充值、消费或退款时，只记录 Apple 账户余额变动，不计银行卡付款部分。「核对实际余额」会比较并保存差额。「撤销最后一条记录」仅撤销本机记录，不撤销 Apple 交易。「导出 CSV」只包含账本，不包含准备事项。

准备事项与提醒

在「换区准备」填写目标地区，编辑事项备注和到期时间，或添加自定义事项。完成或删除事项会取消待触发提醒。若未允许通知，可关闭提醒后保存事项，或到系统设置开启通知。已到期事项仍会保留在清单。勾选完成不代表具备换区资格，请核对 Apple 当前要求。

旧版本购买与待批准请求

本版不发起新付款，旧收据与手动账本独立保存。请保留 App，在「设置」同步历史交易，并核对 Apple 购买记录。旧的待批准请求以后仍可能获批扣款；只有家长或 Apple 确认拒绝或取消后，才归档待批准方案。归档不能取消交易或退款。

数据或账户问题

若无法读取记录，请保留 App 并联系开发者，不要通过卸载修复。若保存失败，请检查设备存储后重试。数据不跨设备同步，不参与 iCloud 备份。账户、余额和退款问题请联系 Apple 支持。

gdutwebber@gmail.com。请说明 App 版本、iOS 版本、语言和问题，不要发送密码、验证码或银行卡资料。

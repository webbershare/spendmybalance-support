Privacy policy

Spend My Balance is an independent utility with no registration, ads or third-party analytics.

Local records

The app stores the currency and opening balance you choose, manually entered credit activity, reconciliation amounts and differences, dates, notes, destination, preparation tasks, due dates, reminder preferences and language. It does not read Apple Account balances or upload these records to a developer server. Journal, task and historical transaction files are excluded from iCloud backup. Uninstalling removes local records; they do not sync across devices.

Exports and notifications

CSV export contains journal amounts, dates and notes, not preparation tasks or Apple transaction identifiers. You choose where to save it; a cloud storage provider you select may receive it under its own terms. Optional reminders use Apple's local notification system. Permission is requested only when you save an enabled reminder. Notification text is generic and does not include your balances, destination or personal notes. No developer push notification server is used.

Earlier-version Apple purchases

This version does not initiate new purchases. It preserves earlier purchase plans, random purchase identifiers and verified receipts. StoreKit may supply unresolved transactions and updates to known records, including product and transaction identifiers, quantities, dates, and refund or revocation status. You can explicitly synchronize with Apple to review earlier purchases. These receipts do not automatically alter the manual journal. We cannot read card details or billing tax information.

Support and websites

External Apple links follow Apple's policies. Copy support message writes only that text to the clipboard; the app does not read it. Support email includes only information you send voluntarily; we use it to respond. Do not send credentials or card details. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to legal retention requirements.

These pages use GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses under its privacy statement. We add no analytics, ads, cookies or contact forms. Website hosting does not upload app records. The app does not request contacts, location, camera or photo access.

隐私政策

「余额清零」是独立第三方工具，无需注册，没有广告或第三方分析服务。

本机记录

App 保存你选择的币种、初始余额、手动收支记录、核对金额与差额、日期、备注、目标地区、准备事项、到期时间、提醒偏好及语言。不读取 Apple 账户余额，不将上述记录上传到开发者服务器。账本、事项与历史交易文件不参与 iCloud 备份，不跨设备同步，卸载会删除本机记录。

导出与通知

CSV 导出包含账本金额、日期和备注，不包含准备事项或 Apple 交易标识。保存位置由你选择；若选择云存储，对应服务商可能按其条款接收文件。可选提醒使用 Apple 本机通知，只在保存已开启的提醒时申请权限。通知采用通用文案，不包含余额、目标地区或私人备注；没有开发者推送服务器。

旧版本 Apple 购买

本版不发起新的购买，保留旧购买方案、随机购买标识与已验证收据。StoreKit 可能返回未完成交易和已知记录的更新，包括商品和交易标识、数量、日期、退款或撤销状态。你可主动与 Apple 同步历史交易。这些收据不会自动改变手动账本。App 无法读取银行卡详情或账单税费信息。

支持与网页

打开 Apple 外部链接适用 Apple 的政策。复制求助文案只向剪贴板写入该文字，不读取剪贴板。支持邮件仅包含你主动提供的信息，用于答复问题；不要发送凭据或银行卡资料。你可申请删除支持邮件，但法律要求保留的信息除外。

网页使用 GitHub Pages 托管，GitHub 可能按照其 隐私声明 处理 IP 地址等技术信息。我们未添加统计、广告、Cookie 或联系表单。托管不会上传 App 记录。App 不申请通讯录、定位、相机或照片权限。

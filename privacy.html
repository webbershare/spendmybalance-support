Privacy policy

Spend My Balance is an independent iPhone utility. You do not need to create an account.

Data on your device

The app stores your selected balance estimate, purchase plan, language preference, an installation identifier and verified transaction records on your device. It does not send these records to our server. The transaction ledger is excluded from iCloud device backups. Uninstalling the app removes its local records.

Apple purchases

Apple processes payments. The app receives product prices and signed transaction information from StoreKit, including product identifiers, quantities, transaction identifiers, dates and refund or revocation status. A random identifier for each purchase attempt is sent to Apple as the purchase account token to match purchases with local progress. We cannot read your Apple Account balance, payment card details or billing tax jurisdiction.

No advertising or analytics

The app does not include advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs, and does not request access to your contacts, location, camera or photos.

Support and external websites

Opening Apple Support or other external links is subject to those services' privacy policies. Copying a support message writes only that message to the system clipboard; the app does not read the clipboard. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Do not send passwords, verification codes or payment card details. You may ask us to delete your support correspondence, subject to applicable legal retention obligations.

Website hosting

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its service, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not add analytics, advertising, cookies or contact forms to these pages. Website hosting does not upload the app's local balance or transaction records.

隐私政策

「余额清零」是一款独立的 iPhone 工具，无需创建账号。

本机数据

App 在设备本地保存所选余额估算、购买方案、语言偏好、安装标识及已验证的交易记录，不将这些记录上传到开发者服务器。交易记录目录不参与 iCloud 设备备份。卸载 App 会删除本地记录。

Apple 购买服务

付款由 Apple 处理。App 通过 StoreKit 获取商品价格和已签名交易信息，包括商品标识、数量、交易编号、日期、退款或撤销状态。每次购买时向 Apple 发送随机生成的购买标识，以关联本机进度。我们无法读取你的 Apple 账户余额、银行卡详情或账单税区。

无广告与分析 SDK

App 不接入广告、跟踪或第三方统计 SDK，不申请通讯录、定位、相机或照片权限。

支持与外部网页

打开 Apple 支持等外部链接后，适用对应服务的隐私政策。复制求助文案只将该文案写入系统剪贴板，App 不读取剪贴板。主动发送支持邮件时，我们会收到你提供的信息，并用于回复问题。不要发送密码、验证码或银行卡资料。你可以申请删除支持邮件，但适用法律要求保留的信息除外。

网站托管

支持页和隐私政策页使用 GitHub Pages 托管。GitHub 可能为提供服务和保障安全收集 IP 地址等技术信息，具体见 GitHub 隐私声明。我们没有在网页中添加统计、广告、Cookie 或联系表单。网页托管不会上传 App 本地保存的余额或交易记录。
